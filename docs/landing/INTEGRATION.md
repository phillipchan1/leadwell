# Integrating the Polarch landing page

Copy `index.html`, `landing.css`, `landing.js` and `assets/` (including `assets/fonts/`) as-is into the Vite repo's `public/landing/` (no bundling, no redesign), and serve `/landing/index.html` at `/`, e.g. with a Vercel rewrite. The two “Continue with Google” buttons link to `href="/signin?google=1"` and the header and footer “Sign in” links to `href="/signin"`, the route PR #7 adds for the app's existing `Login` screen, so nothing else needs wiring. Keep the inline script at the top of `<head>`: it sends signed-in visitors and OAuth callbacks (`?code=`, `#access_token`) on to the app before anything renders. The page makes no third-party requests: the three typefaces are self-hosted Latin subsets in `assets/fonts/`, the first screen's CSS is inlined in `index.html` (keep that `<style>` block in step with `landing.css` if you restyle the header or hero), and the water is a WebGL canvas that `landing.js` adds after load, falling back to a 2D canvas, or to each chapter's plain background color without script. Below the hero, chapters start as `content-visibility: hidden` (only when script runs) and `landing.js` lays them out one per task once the stylesheet and fonts are in, or at the first scroll; if you add a chapter, give it the same `main > section` placement and it's handled.

**What Polarch is, as the page states it:** a quiet instrument for leading people well, at work, at church and at home, that keeps the teams and people you lead in one place: who they are, how they’re doing, whether you’re ready for the next time you sit down, and who you’re carrying in prayer.
