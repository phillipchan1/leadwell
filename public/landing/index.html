10:45Profiles

Read each person the way the assessments already do.

Record someone’s CliftonStrengths top five, Enneagram and MBTI. Strengths show in Gallup’s own domain colors, so a chip here matches every report your people already have. Teams show their domain balance and their blind spots.

Alongside the profile: strengths, watch-outs, how to lead them, goals, dated notes and a topic board. There’s also an AI coach for each person.

11:56Readiness · meetings

You’re sitting down in four minutes. Do you know what this meeting is for?

  • On the booksa next one exists, booked or projected
  • Last one written up
  • Agenda has something queued
  • No overdue commitments

Give a meeting a rhythm and LeadWell projects the next one from the last, so nothing has to be on a calendar for the signal to work. A 1:1, a staff meeting, a practice and a check-in with your boss all prep the same way.

  1. DriftingPast the rhythm, nothing next. Get it on the calendar.
  2. Loose endA meeting happened and was never written up.
  3. Prep dueThe window is open and the checklist isn’t done.
  4. ReadyNothing to do. Show up.
  5. RestingMet recently. Nothing owed, and that’s a good state.
  6. DormantAs-needed, and the floor hasn’t been crossed.

Worst first, on purpose: not having seen someone outranks not having filed the notes. Cards roll up worst-of, never an average, and nothing is measured until you opt in.

17:00Leading up

Leading down, you track their growth. Leading up, you track your value.

A board, an elders group, the one leader you answer to at work: they sit above you in the tree, with the same check-ins, notes and topic board as everyone else. A topic for your boss is an ask, an escalation or a decision you need.

Operating manual

What they reward

Their anxieties

21:30Prayer · who you’re carrying

There is no scale, and there is no score.

Taking someone up in prayer is a deliberate act, and so is laying them down. LeadWell only measures the one honest thing it can: how long it’s been.

  • Prayed this weekFilled.
  • CarryingOutlined.
  • Gone quietDashed, after 21 days. The only one that asks anything of you.
  • AnsweredGilt. Never “done”.

Praying for four people out of forty is a true answer. Not carrying is never counted as a gap.
