Arcs, and an agenda
you share.

Meghan and I are working through Crucial Conversations in our 1:1s. That plan should live somewhere. Each meeting should already know which chapter it's on. And Meghan should be able to see where we're going and add her own items.

The design has two parts. An arc is something you work through together, one step per meeting. It follows your pace rather than a fixed calendar. Sharing applies to a meeting, not a team, so the agenda is shared and your own read of the person stays private.

1

Adding is the first thing on the page

She picks a section and a week, or just presses Add, and the item goes onto the next meeting under Anything else. It appears on Phil's agenda straight away with her face on it.

2

The book has its own card

It shows what to read before Thursday and lets her tick off last week's practice. Read → Talk → Try is where the actual change happens. Both people can see that loop, so both keep it going.

3

Coming up shows where the meetings are heading

She sees the next three meetings, including skipped weeks and the chapter each one covers. Nothing in it is private.

4

What's left out matters as much

She never sees health, prayer, assessments, the Ideas tray, prep, My notes, the locked margin item, or any other meeting. The guest page uses its own server routes and never selects those columns.

5

If she uses Polarch herself

Accepting the invite adds the meeting to her workspace as an attend meeting with Phil. Each of them prepares in a private space, and the agenda they meet over is shared.

D · The sharing model

Share the meeting, not the team.

Adding Meghan to a team would mean deciding, one field at a time, which of your private reads she sees, and every answer leaks something. Topics, follow-ups and occurrences already belong to the meeting, and role: convene | attend already exists. The meeting is the unit Polarch was built around.

The room · shared
  • The next meeting's running order
  • Topics on shared weeks, which either person can add
  • The arc: steps, Read / Talk / Try, and both takeaways
  • Follow-ups, each with an owner
  • Our notes in the write-up
  • Plan ahead: upcoming weeks and the ribbon
Your read · always private
  • Health, prayer, assessments, how to lead
  • The Ideas tray, for anything not yet placed
  • Prep checks and readiness
  • My notes in the write-up (everything written so far)
  • Any topic you lock
  • Every other meeting and person
Placing an item on a week shares it.

An idea stays private in your tray. Once you put it on a week of a shared meeting, it's on the agenda you both see. One click on the lock keeps a specific item yours. Shared items are always labelled, so you can always tell who will see what.

F · The closing page

A record of what you learned together.

When the last step is done, the arc closes into one page with every takeaway from both of you, in order. You can print it or send it to her.
