Everything is set at the same volume

Polarch has the right features. What it lacks is rank. Terracotta means "you can press this", "this is the brand", "this is now" and "this is a problem" all at once, so it lands on ten things per screen. Small uppercase labels announce every block, mono type makes dates look like alerts, and help text sits on screen long after you've learned it. The fix is to give each screen one loud thing and let the rest go quiet.

Who this is for

You lead people in three places at once: a product team at work, a church staff and its ministries, and the people at home. You aren't running a CRM. You're trying to walk into each conversation ready and leave nobody behind. Here is what you actually do, ranked by how often you do it.

many / dayCatch a thought about someone"Raise the vacation with Dennis." Two seconds, then it's filed to the right meeting.
dailyKnow who's next and whether you're readyOne answer: the next meeting and what's on it.
few / weekRun the meetingAgenda on the left, notes in the middle, nothing else asking for you.
weeklyPlan aheadDrop ideas onto future weeks.
weeklyNotice who's slipping, and praySomeone has drifted. Someone you're carrying has gone quiet.
rarelySet things upRhythms, sharing, arcs, settings. These should be one click away, never on the main path.

So the app comes down to three verbs: catch, prepare and run. Every screen should open on one of them. Everything else is reference, so it can sit quietly until you go looking.

Eight rules for the whole app

One loud thing per screenOnly one filled button per view. Everything else is ink text or an icon.
Color means state, never "pressable"Buttons and links are ink. Green, amber and red are only for health and readiness. Violet is only for prayer.
The star means nowTerracotta stays in the mark and marks what is live or today: the live pill and the next meeting. Nothing else gets it.
Only label what can't speak for itselfA date doesn't need "NEXT UP" above it. Section labels are sentence case, quiet, and used only where you have to scan.
Say it onceEach fact appears in one place. The rhythm goes in the subtitle, not in the subtitle, a stat card and the board.
Icons for repeat verbs, words for the main oneShare, add a date, more, close: these become icons with tooltips. The one primary verb keeps its words.
Mono for numbers in columnsUse it for countdowns that line up, and never in caps. "WED · TODAY" becomes "Today".
Show help on empty or on hover"Drag an idea onto a week…" shows once, on an empty board or behind a ? icon. It doesn't sit there forever.

Motion: a calm machine

Everything arrives and nothing pops. One easing curve, three durations, and no loops except for something that is actually live.

Screen changeThe new screen rises 6px and fades in. The old one doesn't animate out, so nothing waits.260ms
Tab changeThe underline slides to the new tab, and the panel below fades up.220ms
Lists and cardsThe first view staggers in, 30–40ms per item and capped at 8, so long lists don't drag.360ms
HoverCards lift 1px with a softer shadow, and row actions fade in.140ms
PressButtons scale to 0.97 and spring back.90ms
LiveThe live dot breathes slowly. It's the only looping animation in the app.2.4s
DoneThe check draws itself, the row settles, then it collapses.300ms

What this first pass changes

These are app-wide changes, so every screen gets quieter at once. Each screen's cuts above come next, one screen at a time.

  • Ink replaces terracotta for anything pressable. It's one token block in brand.css, so every button, link, selected state and focus ring goes charcoal, or pale in dark mode. The star keeps the mark, the live pill and "today".
  • Section labels stop shouting. The eyebrow utility is now sentence case at normal tracking, across all 38 uses.
  • Motion tokens and entrances. Screens and entity tabs rise in on change, the tab underline slides, buttons press, and it all respects reduced motion.
  • The meeting header. Share and one-off date are icons, Start is the one primary button, the boxed stat cards became one status line, and "you convene" is gone.
  • The dark frame. The capture + and Run's Done button are pale ink, the selected place is white, the badge counts are muted, and "Today" in Up next is the one star-colored word. The live dot breathes slowly instead of blinking red.

To undo the color change, restore the press-ramp block at the top of src/styles/brand.css. Nothing else depends on it.
