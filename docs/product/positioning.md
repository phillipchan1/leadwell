## Competitive alternatives

What a Portfolio Leader actually does today:

| Alternative | Why it wins | Why it loses |
|---|---|---|
| **Notes app + calendar** (the real competitor) | Free, instant, already open, zero learning | Nothing accumulates; no retrieval at the moment of need; can't see across contexts |
| **Their own memory** | Effortless, feels sufficient | Decays; biased toward the loud and the recent; fails exactly when stakes are highest |
| **1:1 tools** (Lattice, 15Five, Fellow, Culture Amp) | Purpose-built, polished, integrated | Company-owned and company-visible → kills candor; one org only; assume formal authority; nothing for church/volunteer/peer/leading-up |
| **A CRM bent into shape** (Notion, Airtable, Monday) | Infinitely flexible | You build and maintain it yourself; no domain intelligence; flexibility is a tax, not a feature |
| **Generic AI chat** | Free, conversational, immediate | No memory of your people; gives leadership advice, not advice about *this person* |
| **Executive coach** | Genuinely excellent | $300+/hr, weekly at best, isn't there at 8:55am |

## Unique attributes

Things that are true of LeadWell and not of the alternatives:

1. **Every context on one canvas.** Work, church, family, community — one map.
   Company-owned tools structurally cannot follow us here.
2. **Four leadership capacities as a first-class model.** Manager, Leader,
   Influence, Report up — with the guidance changing accordingly.
3. **Leading up as a real discipline** with its own artifacts: operating
   manual, currency, their scorecard, wins ledger. Effectively unoccupied
   ground.
4. **Assessment frameworks converted into action**, not filed. Full
   CliftonStrengths 34 mapped to Gallup domains, plus Enneagram and MBTI.
5. **Private by construction.** Owned by the leader, not the employer. Nobody
   above them can read it.
6. **Context-loaded AI.** The coach reads the whole profile before answering.

## Market frame

**We are best in a market that doesn't have a name yet.** The nearest label is
*personal leadership OS* — the person-centered counterpart to the personal
knowledge management category (Obsidian, Roam, Notion for thinking; this, for
people).

The strategic reason not to enter as "1:1 software": that market is defined by
company-owned tools bought by HR, where our defining attributes (private,
personal, multi-context) read as *missing enterprise features* rather than as
the point. Enter that frame and we lose on their axes.

**The wedge:** leading up. It's the most acute, least-served, most emotionally
charged job, and it needs zero buy-in from anyone else — no team to onboard, no
org to convince. Land there, expand into leading down.

## Positioning statement

> For leaders who carry people across more than one part of their life —
> a day job, a church or nonprofit, a peer group, and the people they report to
> — LeadWell is a private leadership system of record.
>
> Unlike 1:1 tools owned by your employer, it holds every context on one map,
> knows the difference between the four kinds of authority you actually hold,
> and gives you a coach that has read everything you know about the specific
> person in front of you.
