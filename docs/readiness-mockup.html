Readiness

Knowing whether I'm behind — before I sit down, not four minutes into the meeting.

The gap

LeadWell answers “who do I know?” — coverage, blind spots, domain mix. That's a library metric. It's backward-looking, and it reads the same on Tuesday as it does on Wednesday.

It cannot tell you that tomorrow's 9am has no agenda and last week's never got written up. Coverage says the shelf is stocked. It says nothing about whether you're ready to pull from it.

Behind isn't a score. Behind is: the prep window is open and the checklist isn't done.

The split

Two kinds of behind, two opposite fixes

Groomed is the right idea aimed at the wrong half. A backlog is groomed when the next sprint's items are ready to pull — not when the backlog is well documented. Grooming is about the next pull, and it decays. So the concept splits in two, and the split is the whole design.

You can be full Depth and zero Prep — you know Marcus cold and you'll still walk in naked. Blend them into one “groomed %” and you lose which kind of behind you are.

The ladder

Five states, named — not a percentage

A number on a canvas card is dashboard sludge. A state is a decision. Order matters: not having seen a human outranks not having filed the notes. That precedence is the leadership opinion baked into the code.

Resting

Met recently. The window hasn't opened.

Do nothing. Zero prep right after a 1:1 is correct, not a failure.

Ready

Window open, checklist done.

Show up.

Prep due

Window open, something's missing.

Ten minutes of grooming. The drawer tells you which ten.

Loose end

The meeting happened and never got written up, or a commitment of mine is overdue.

Close the loop. Paperwork debt.

Drifting

Past cadence with nothing on the calendar.

Book it. Relationship debt — always sorted above paperwork.

Two modifiers, not states: Unread (no leadership read — already shown by avatar dimming) and Paused (people you don't run 1:1s with, excluded entirely). Paused is load-bearing: without it a volunteer team sits permanently red and you learn to ignore the whole layer.

Layer R. The canvas already toggles layers by keystroke — readiness joins as R, so “readiness across Church only” is two keys. Off, cards read exactly as they do today. On, the left rail takes the worst member's state, the chip counts down to the next meeting, and every person row's accent bar recolors. Toggle it above.

The clock

Prep is measured against a window, not a calendar

An empty agenda twelve days out is fine. An empty agenda tomorrow is an emergency. Score prep without the clock and it's either permanently red or meaningless. The window opens at max(2 days, 25% of cadence) — about two days out on a weekly 1:1, a week out on a monthly team.

Before the window: Resting — nothing is owed. Inside it, an incomplete checklist becomes Prep due. Green has to be reachable, and it has to be reachable most of the time, or the one real alarm gets ignored along with the noise.

Drafts the agenda from open actions, last notes, goals, and her profile

The checklist is the score. No hidden weights, no mystery number — six named things, each one click from fixed. A score you can't act on is just guilt with a progress bar.

✦ Prep me is the 8:59am moment. The AI seam already exists; this points it at the one question that matters right now — what is this meeting for? — with her open actions, last write-up, goals, and read as context. Ninety seconds, and you're not naked.

The ritual matters more than the meter

Grooming works in agile because it's a scheduled ceremony, not an ambient guilt gauge. The Prep Sweep is a worst-first list of everything whose window opens this week, with inline agenda fields. Twenty minutes on Monday and the canvas goes green.

The score tells you that you're behind. The sweep is what stops you being behind. Ship both or ship neither.

Getting there

Three phases, and the first one stands alone

PHASE 1

The read

Derive readiness from data already in the store, plus nextMeeting and a numeric cadence. Layer R, rail, countdown chip, header line.

Answers “am I behind” without changing how anything is entered.

PHASE 2

The fix

Prep drawer with the live checklist. Per-person cadence, paused, and ✦ Prep me.

This is what makes it stick.

PHASE 3

The rhythm

Prep Sweep view. Depth goes stale after twelve months and asks to be re-verified. Manager check-in dates and Wins-ledger recency.

Leading up gets the same discipline as leading down.

Principles
  • Only measure what you'd act on. Everything else is guilt generation.
  • Green must be reachable, or the one real alarm gets ignored with the noise.
  • Decay, don't punish. Resting is a good state.
  • Relationship debt outranks paperwork debt. Always.
  • Depth is three coarse tiers, never a percentage of fields filled — a percentage teaches you to fill boxes to turn something green.
